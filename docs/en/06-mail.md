# 6. Mail

[← Passports](05-passports.md) · [Contents](README.md) · [Next: Newspapers →](07-newspapers.md)

## Envelope and letter

1. Craft an **Envelope** (5 paper → 2 envelopes) and **right-click** with it.
2. Write the letter and enter the **recipient**.
3. **Attachments:** up to **3** documents, passports or newspapers. Hold the attachment in your off hand and press "Put in from other hand".
4. **Seal it with wax** (you need Sealing Wax in your inventory). Once sealed, the letter can't be changed.

### Sealing wax and impressions
- **Sealing Wax:** honeycomb + red dye (makes 4).
- If you have an **organization stamp** in your inventory, its impression is pressed into the wax. A personal stamp doesn't work for this.
- If the envelope is opened, the wax is **broken**, and the recipient will see it.
- An unsealed envelope is marked: "anyone could have read it".

## Sending: the mailbox

The **Mailbox** (`III` / `ICI` / ` I `: iron and a chest) is the block that sends and receives letters.

- Walk up to any mailbox with the letter in hand and **right-click** to send it.
- The recipient collects mail **from any mailbox**.
- On login, players get a notice when letters are waiting.

## Several recipients and mailing lists

The recipient field accepts:

| Entry | Who gets it |
|---|---|
| `Alex, Sam` | Several players — names separated by commas |
| `@CityHall` | Every member of the organization |
| `@list_name` | A saved mailing list |
| `@subscribers` | A newspaper's subscribers (only when mailing a newspaper) |

Names and lists are suggested as you type. Mass mailing to many people needs `signedsealed.mail.mass` (OP only by default).

**Mailing lists:** `/seal mail lists` lets you create and rename lists and add people.

## Delivery receipts

The sender is notified when the letter is **picked up** from a mailbox, so you know an important document arrived.

## Alternative: the computer inbox

You can also send documents without envelopes through a [computer](08-office.md#inbox): originals or certified copies, with an optional signature request.
